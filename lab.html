Private Crypto Lab

The Private Crypto Lab focuses on the design and security analysis of symmetric cryptographic primitives. Our research investigates the structural properties of block and stream ciphers, with an emphasis on developing advanced cryptanalytic techniques.

We study quantum cryptanalysis to understand the impact of quantum adversaries on symmetric-key security. In parallel, we explore the application of machine learning methods to cryptanalysis, bridging data-driven techniques with classical approaches.
